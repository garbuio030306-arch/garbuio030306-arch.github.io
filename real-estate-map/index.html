How to read this map

This site has 5 pages, or "views". Each one looks at the same research a different way.

  • Full Picture — the research drawn as a house. Click a room to walk in and see what's inside, then click any item to read it. Use the search bar to jump straight to anything.
  • Scenario Explorer — pick a factor and a "what if" to see what happens.
  • Vertical Value Chain — how a building gets made and owned, step by step.
  • Interconnection Network — a map of which factors affect which others. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Factor × Segment Heatmap — a grid showing which factors affect which parts of real estate.

Colours: notes = from your research PDF, added = we looked it up and cited a source, proposed = our guess, for you to check. Use the "Hide added/proposed" switches at the top to see notes only.

Keyboard: Tab to move between controls, Enter or Space to select, Escape to close.
