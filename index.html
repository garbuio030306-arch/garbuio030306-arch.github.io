Fixed income2026

US Treasury Yield Curve Forecaster

An interactive 24-month forecast of the 3-month, 2-year and 10-year Treasury yields. Move macro levers (UK, Japan and German yields, US unemployment, FX, credit spreads, VIX) and watch the curve, the term spreads and the implied recession probability respond.

  • Ensemble of VARX, XGBoost and Diebold-Li, weighted by walk-forward backtest error
  • GARCH uncertainty bands and an Estrella-Mishkin recession probit (out-of-sample AUC 0.83)
  • Every model is scored against a random walk, with economic sign checks on each lever
  • Python
  • VARX
  • XGBoost
  • Nelson-Siegel
  • GARCH
  • FRED
